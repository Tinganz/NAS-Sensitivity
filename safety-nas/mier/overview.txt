Quick summary for me to remember/understand the pipeline:
1. We have a car with 1-D lidar, and three cnns:
    a. left_wall_dist: how far from left wall 
    b. track_width: how wide the track is
    c. heading_error: misorientation from track center
2. We previously hard coded five architectures (using only 3 here bc size too small)
    a. We run a NAS to find three better CNNs than the baseline with a size constraint.

Pipeline:
1. Search in 120 candidates:
    a. Train 3 CNN models for 60 epochs each
    b. Evaluate these 3 models on 3 tracks (Sepang, Austin, Melbourne) and averages RMSE
    c. Penalize architecture that has parameter number way far from size constraint
    d. I will put trials to safety-nas/mier/output/search/nas_trials_session_id.jsonl

2. Retrain
    a. Reads the .jsonl, get trails that fits our budget (abs_overflow < 0.1), pick lowest RMSE, and retrain:
        i. 150 epochs
    b. Save three .pt checkpoint files to safety-nas/mier/output/retrain/<hash>/
        i. safety-nas/mier/output/retrain/<hash>/left_wall_dist_arch8_trailN.pt
        ii. safety-nas/mier/output/retrain/<hash>/track_width_arch8_trialN.pt
        iii. safety-nas/mier/output/retrain/<hash>/heading_error_arch8_trial.pt

3. Compare
    a. Run the simulator on 7 race tracks, for each track it drives one lap with:
        i. NAS results
        ii. baseline
    b. Records RMSE, heading error, wall clearance
    c. output: safety-nas/mier/output/compare/<hash>/metrics.jsonl
